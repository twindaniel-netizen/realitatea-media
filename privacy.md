# Politica de confidentialitate - YouTube Upload (Realitatea Media)

Ultima actualizare: 19 noiembrie 2026

Aplicatia "YouTube Upload" este un instrument intern, folosit exclusiv de administratorul canalului YouTube Realitatea Media (https://www.youtube.com/@realitateamedia2026) pentru a incarca videoclipuri pe propriul canal, folosind YouTube Data API.

## Ce acces solicita aplicatia
Aplicatia solicita permisiunea de a incarca videoclipuri pe canalul YouTube al administratorului (scope: youtube.upload) si de a citi identitatea canalului propriu (youtube.readonly), doar pentru a verifica ca incarcarea se face pe canalul corect.

## Ce date colectam
Nu colectam, nu vindem si nu transmitem catre terti date personale. Aplicatia nu are alti utilizatori in afara administratorului. Singurele date folosite sunt videoclipurile pe care administratorul alege sa le incarce si un token de autorizare, salvat local pe calculatorul administratorului. Tokenul nu este trimis nicaieri in afara serverelor Google.

## Cum sunt folosite datele
Datele sunt folosite doar pentru a incarca videoclipuri pe canalul propriu. Utilizarea informatiilor primite de la Google API respecta Google API Services User Data Policy, inclusiv cerintele de Limited Use.

## Stergerea datelor si revocarea accesului
Administratorul poate revoca oricand accesul din https://myaccount.google.com/permissions . Dupa revocare, tokenul local devine invalid. Nu stocam alte date pe servere proprii.

## Contact
Pentru intrebari, folositi pagina canalului: https://www.youtube.com/@realitateamedia2026

# Privacy Policy - YouTube Upload (Realitatea Media)

Last updated: November 19, 2026

"YouTube Upload" is an internal tool used solely by the administrator of the Realitatea Media YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@realitateamedia2026) to upload videos to the administrator's own channel through the YouTube Data API.

## Access requested
The app requests permission to upload videos to the administrator's own YouTube channel (scope: youtube.upload) and to read the identity of that channel (youtube.readonly), only to confirm that uploads go to the correct channel.

## Data we collect
We do not collect, sell or share personal data with third parties. The app has no users other than the administrator. The only data used are the videos the administrator chooses to upload and an authorization token stored locally on the administrator's computer. The token is never sent anywhere other than Google's servers.

## How data is used
Data is used only to upload videos to the administrator's own channel. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Data deletion and revoking access
The administrator can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions . After revocation the local token becomes invalid. We store no other data on our own servers.

## Contact
For questions, use the channel page: https://www.youtube.com/@realitateamedia2026
